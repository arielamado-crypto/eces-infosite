Message from the Principal

Together, we build bright futures.

“Every child who walks through our gates carries a dream. Our duty is to help each one discover it, believe in it, and reach it.”

Welcome to Estancia Central Elementary School. As a Special Science Elementary School, we take pride in nurturing learners who are curious, creative and caring — learners who love to ask questions and find answers.

None of this is possible without our parents, teachers and partners in the community. Thank you for your trust and support. Let us continue working hand in hand for the success of every learner.
