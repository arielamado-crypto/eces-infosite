An enriched curriculum in science and mathematics with investigatory projects, science fairs and quiz bees for gifted and interested learners.

Computer classes, typing practice and digital learning tools that build 21st-century skills.

MATATAG Curriculum

A decongested curriculum focused on foundational skills, values and learner well-being.
